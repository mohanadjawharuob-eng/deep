Apps

Four small tools that run entirely on your device. No account, no server, nothing sent anywhere — everything you enter stays in your browser's storage on the phone or computer you entered it on.

DaybookTasks, today's focus and habit streaks › CofferBudgets, goals, debts and a plain-text ledger › KitchenPlan the week, get one merged shopping list › TimesheetTrack hours per client, then invoice them ›
Install one to your home screen
  1. Open the app itself (tap it above) — not this page.
  2. Chrome menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen. On iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. It gets its own icon and opens full-screen, without the browser bar.

Each app stores its data separately per device. Use the Export backup button in each app's settings if the data matters to you.
